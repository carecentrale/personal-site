About

Who I am

I started as a developer, moved into product, and I still think like one. I like the details: how a settlement file moves, why a pricing rule breaks, what a merchant really needs from a terminal. Most of my career has been in fintech and payments, in Montreal and remotely from Quebec.

I'm bilingual in French and English, and I enjoy working across teams, from engineering and operations to sales and the people who sign the contracts.

Outside of work I sit on the board of a non-profit, and I mentor people in the Chatfuel and LinkedIn communities who are moving into product or AI roles.

Leading people

Leading and building teams

Most of my jobs have meant running product managers and scrum teams at the same time. Over the years that has ranged from a small group of two or four product managers to a roadmap spread across eight teams.

What I care about is clear ownership. Everyone should know what they're responsible for, why it matters, and how it ties to the business. I also like building the team itself: hiring, coaching junior product people, and setting up the rhythm (discovery, planning, reviews) so the group can run without me in every meeting.

AI in my work

Building with agentic AI

I use AI every day, and I've started building with it too. The most useful thing I've found is turning messy inputs (regulator specs, processor docs, meeting transcripts) into requirements a team can actually build from. In one case that took a month from start to a working demo.

I also build AI into my side projects, and I'm more interested in the parts that need human judgment than in the parts that can be automated. Good output needs a person checking it against real facts, so I design for that.
